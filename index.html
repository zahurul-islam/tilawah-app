FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the Nurani course really free?

Yes. The full Nurani course (Arabic letters, harakat, tajweed, Juz Amma and practice tests) and the Mushaf reader are always free.

What do I get with Tilawah Pro?

Pro unlocks Quran recitation with AI feedback and Hifz mode. It costs $2.99 per month or $29.99 per year.

Is my voice recorded?

No. Speech recognition runs on your device. We never record, store or receive your voice; only the recognized words and results are saved. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Can the AI replace a Quran teacher?

No. Feedback is produced automatically and can be wrong. It is a learning aid; please confirm your tajweed and memorization with a qualified teacher.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Step-by-step instructions are on the Support page.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account, or email tilwaapp@gmail.com. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription.
