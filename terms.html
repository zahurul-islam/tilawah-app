2. The service

Tilawah helps you read, learn and memorize the Quran. It includes a Quran reader with translations, tafsir and recitation audio; the Nurani course (Arabic letters, harakat, tajweed and practice tests); recitation practice with automatic feedback; and memorization (Hifz) tools.

3. Your account

You can sign in with email and password, Google or Apple. Keep your sign-in details private; you are responsible for activity on your account. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account.

4. Tilawah Pro

  • The Recite section (listening, practice and memorization with automatic feedback) and Hifz mode require a Tilawah Pro subscription. The Quran reader and the Nurani course are free.
  • Payment is charged to your App Store or Google Play account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.
  • If a free trial is offered, it becomes a paid subscription when it ends unless you cancel before then.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Deleting your Tilawah account does not cancel the subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

5. Recitation feedback is a learning aid

Feedback on your recitation is produced automatically by speech recognition and can be wrong. It does not replace learning from a qualified teacher; please confirm your tajweed and memorization with one.
