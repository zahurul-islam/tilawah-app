Support

Questions, bug reports or feature requests: email tilwaapp@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Manage or cancel your subscription

On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.

Restore purchases

In the app, open the paywall and tap Restore.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Settings, Delete account. You can also email tilwaapp@gmail.com from your account email to request deletion; we delete your account data within 30 days.
