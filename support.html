Support

Questions, bug reports or feature requests? We are here to help.

Contact us

Questions, bug reports or feature requests: email tilwaapp@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Email support

Manage or cancel your subscription

  • iPhoneSettings›your name›Subscriptions
  • AndroidGoogle Play›Payments and subscriptions›Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.

Restore purchases

Changed phones or reinstalled the app? In the app, open the paywall and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you used to subscribe.

Delete your account and data

  • In the appSettings›Delete account
  • By emailEmail tilwaapp@gmail.com from your account email to request deletion; we delete your account data within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play settings.
