Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what the Tilawah app ("the App", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers. Tilawah uses the Quran Foundation APIs (Quran.com) to show Quran text, translations, tafsir and recitations; that content is retrieved without collecting personal data about you.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the name and profile photo they provide; your app language; when your account was created and last used. If you hide your email with Sign in with Apple, we receive only Apple's relay address.
  • Learning data, only if you turn on sync: completed lessons and scores, practice-test results, points, streaks, badges, bookmarks, and the outcome of each recitation session (which ayat, the words recognized, mistakes found, accuracy and duration), so your history is available on all your devices. Because Quran learning data can reveal your religious beliefs, we treat it as sensitive and store it in your account only with your explicit consent. We ask for it separately when you sign up, and you can turn sync on or off at any time in Settings. Without consent, this data stays only on your device.
  • Subscription status: whether Tilawah Pro is active and when it expires. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
  • Email verification: a one-time code, stored only as a hash and valid for 10 minutes.

2. Your voice

When you recite, speech recognition runs on your device, using a model built into the App. If that model cannot run on your device, the App uses your phone's own speech service instead: Google's speech recognition on Android, or Apple's on iPhone. Those services may send the audio to Google's or Apple's servers to transcribe it, under their privacy policies. We never record, store or receive your voice; only the recognized words and the results described above are saved.

3. On your device

Settings, downloaded content and progress you make while signed out are stored on your device and removed when you uninstall the App.

4. How we use it

To run your account and sync your progress, verify your email, unlock Tilawah Pro, and keep the App secure and working. We process your account and subscription data because it is needed to provide the App you asked for, and your learning data only with your explicit consent. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

5. Who processes it

We share data only with services that run the App for us:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, database, file storage and server functions): your account and, if you turned on sync, your learning data — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple, if you choose them — policies.google.com/privacy, apple.com/legal/privacy
  • RevenueCat, the App Store and Google Play: process and verify subscriptions; RevenueCat receives your account ID and purchase history — revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Gmail: sends verification codes to your email address
  • Quran.com, EveryAyah.com and MP3Quran: serve Quran text, translations, tafsir and audio; your device contacts them directly, so they see your IP address as any website would

Each service processes data under its own privacy policy and our agreements with it. Your data may be stored on servers outside your country, including in the United States, protected by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. Delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account: this immediately and permanently deletes your profile, learning data and verification records from our database and removes your sign-in. Turning off sync stops new learning data from being uploaded; data already in your account stays until you delete your account. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat follow their own policies; contact us if you want us to ask RevenueCat to delete yours.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to its use. Email tilwaapp@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 or older. The App asks your year of birth when you sign up, uses it only for this check and does not store it. A parent or guardian may create an account and let their child use the App under their supervision. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it.

9. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and is encrypted at rest by Google Cloud, and our database rules let only you read and change your own data.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when the App changes; the date above shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the App.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: tilwaapp@gmail.com
